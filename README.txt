Bring your home's live status to your TRMNL e-ink display. This app reads your Homey's devices, zones and energy data and sends it to your TRMNL screen, so you can see your power usage, indoor climate and what is on or off at a glance, on a calm always-on display instead of reaching for your phone.

Set it up once by entering your TRMNL push address in the app settings. Your screen then stays up to date automatically as things change around the house, on a timer and whenever a device updates.

On the TRMNL side you can pick from ready-made Homey screens for energy, climate, home status and zones, or fork any of them and build the layout you actually want. The screens and this app are both open source.

Setup guide: https://help.trmnl.com/en/articles/16527414-homey
Source code: https://github.com/usetrmnl/trmnl-homey-app
